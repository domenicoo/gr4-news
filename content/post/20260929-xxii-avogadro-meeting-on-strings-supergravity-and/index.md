# XXII Avogadro Meeting on Strings, Supergravity and Gauge Theories

The XXII Avogadro Meeting on Strings, Supergravity and Gauge Theories will take place in Genoa (Italy), on December 16–18, 2026.

The program will include five extended lectures with discussions, one gong-show session and one poster session. Young researchers are especially encouraged to apply for the gong-show and poster sessions. The deadline for registration is November 15, 2026.

### Lecture Program

**1) Cobordism Applications to Physics and Quantum Gravity**
Speakers: Roberta Angius (U. of Hamburg) and Salvatore Raucci (IFT Madrid)

**2) Matrix models and the gauge/gravity duality**
Speakers: Jackson Fliss (ULB) and Edward Mazenc (ETH)

**3) New Perspectives on Anomalies**
Speakers: Andrea Antinucci (Oxford) and Lea Bottini (IHES)

**4) Quantum Field Theory in AdS**
Speakers: Fabiana de Cesare (U. of Turin) and Manuel Loparco (U. of Turin)

**5) String amplitudes**
Speakers: Maurizio Firrotta (Fudan U.) and Maria Nochi (SCGP)

Registration is already open. Please note that registrations will be open until the deadline or until capacity is reached. Priority will be given to PhD students and early-career researchers.

As in previous editions, we can only cover the costs of the social dinner for a limited number of participants. These will be decided based on the order of registration and are contingent on attendance to the meeting for all three days.
