# Cosmology on the steep rise

We are pleased to announce the workshop **"Cosmology on the steep rise"** ([https://www.sexten-cfa.eu/event/csr27/](https://www.sexten-cfa.eu/event/csr27/)) which will be held at the Sexten Center for Astrophysics Riccardo Giacconi, Sesto, Bolzano, Italy, on March 1-5, 2027.

The aim of the workshop is to discuss the status of the latest cosmological measurements, new methodologies of data analysis and novel theoretical developments.

The format includes invited talks, contributed talks, posters, also presented as fire talks, and organized discussions.

* **Submission of contributions deadline:** October 31st, 2026
* **Registration deadline:** November 30th, 2026

**Registration link:** [https://www.sexten-cfa.eu/event/csr27/](https://www.sexten-cfa.eu/event/csr27/)

Please contact cosmology.steep.rise@gmail.com for any inquiry.
