# International Doctorate School "Frontiers in Nuclear and Hadronic Physics 2027" (FNHP2027)

Registration for the International Doctorate School "Frontiers in Nuclear and Hadronic Physics 2027" (FNHP2027) is now open.

The school will take place at the **Galileo Galilei Institute (GGI) in Florence, from February 15 to February 26, 2027**. The school is primarily addressed to Ph.D. students in Theoretical Nuclear Physics. Participation of experimentalists and post-doc fellows is also encouraged.

The 2027 edition will provide a pedagogical introduction to the basic concepts and tools needed for carrying out cutting edge research in nuclear structure, nuclear reactions and nuclear astrophysics. The lectures will emphasize the intimate connections among these fields, paying attention also to the interpretation of experimental data and to applications. Various theoretical frameworks for the study of nuclear systems will be presented, including both ab initio and more phenomenological approaches.

### Program

* **NICOLAS CHAMEL** (Université Libre, Bruxelles): Phases of dense matter in neutron stars and their observational manifestations
* **GIAMPAOLO CO'** (Lecce University): Concepts in quantum many-body physics
* **YIFEI NIU** (Jiao Tong University, Shanghai): Collective excitations and beta decay
* **GREGORY POTEL** (Sevilla University): Direct nuclear reactions: theory and experiment
* **ALEXANDER TICHAI** (Technical University, Darmstadt): Ab initio approaches to nuclear structure

### Logistics and Support

We will admit about 50 students. Lunches during the week-days will be offered on site, free of charge, to all participants. Financial support for accommodation expenses may be provided, upon request, to a selected number of participants (about 30). Accommodation in double rooms will be offered to the other participants at the approximate cost of 40 Euro/day.

### Deadlines and Application

* **Application deadline:** November 30, 2026
* **Acceptance communication:** December 15, 2026

More information and the application form can be found on the website: [https://www.ggi.infn.it/single-school.php?id=579](https://www.ggi.infn.it/single-school.php?id=579)
