Dear Colleagues,

We are pleased to announce the upcoming conference:

**Bi-Hamiltonian structures, Franco Magri and all that**  
October 15–16, 2026, Lecce, Italy

We will celebrate the foundational contributions and active developments in bi-Hamiltonian geometry and integrable systems, on the occasion of Franco Magri's 80th birthday.

**Website:** <https://l.infn.it/magri80>

### Invited Speakers
- G. Falqui (Università di Milano-Bicocca)
- E.V. Ferapontov (Loughborough University, UK)
- A. Ibort (Universidad Carlos III, Madrid)
- P. Lorenzoni (Università di Milano-Bicocca)
- G. Marmo (INFN Sez. Napoli)
- G. Ortenzi (Università di Torino)
- M. Pedroni (Università di Bergamo)
- P. Santini (INFN Sez. Roma)
- G. Tondo (INFN Sez. Lecce)
- A.P. Veselov (Loughborough University, UK)

### Registration & Participation
Participation is free, but registration is mandatory. To register, please send an email to **magri80@unisalento.it**.  
*(Please note: Participation as a speaker is by invitation only.)*
