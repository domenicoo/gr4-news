# Third Advanced Hackathon on Machine Learning

The AI_INFN CSN5 Initiative invites INFN affiliates to the Third Advanced Hackathon on Machine Learning.

**Location:** Ferrara
**Dates:** November 30th to December 3rd, 2026
**Agenda:** [https://agenda.infn.it/event/53025](https://agenda.infn.it/event/53025)

**Registration Information**
Registrations open from Wednesday, September 30th at 9:00 am until Monday, October 12th at 11:59 pm. Please note that if applications exceed available seats, participants will be selected based on criteria including the order of registration.

**Topics**
The agenda includes theoretical lectures and practical hands-on sessions regarding:
* Graph Neural Networks (GNN) applied to Medical Physics
* Quantum Machine Learning
* Neural Networks for integration of dynamical systems
* Graph Neural Network e Transformer for NA62 tracking

The afternoons of Tuesday the 1st and Wednesday the 2nd will be entirely dedicated to guided work on these topics using hardware accelerators provisioned through the distributed DataCloud infrastructure at the INFN sites of CNAF, ReCaS Bari and Milano Bicocca.

**Questions and Comments**
Organizers can be reached at: ml-infn-hackathons@lists.infn.it

# CALL FOR ABSTRACT/PRESENTATION

We invite submissions for educational seminars that emphasize the practical application of AI/ML techniques to real-world physics problems. Relevant topics include High Energy Physics, Gravitational Waves, Medical Physics, and other INFN activities in related physics areas and interdisciplinary research.

We are particularly interested in presentations that explore emerging methodologies such as:
* Applications of Deep Learning to Physics
* Foundation Models
* Fast Simulation
* Uncertainty Quantification
* Data Management strategies, including Open Dataset release and quality control
* MLOps best practices
* Quantum Computing
* Agentic workflows: guidelines and setup

We also welcome proposals on emerging topics and novel applications that demonstrate significant potential for the INFN research community. At the time of submission, please outline the physics domain, the potential benefits of adopting machine learning, and the pedagogical value of the proposed contribution.

**Submission Details**
Abstracts must be submitted through the dedicated Indico form: [https://agenda.infn.it/event/53025/abstracts/](https://agenda.infn.it/event/53025/abstracts/)

* **Abstract Length:** 200-250 words
* **Submission Deadline:** October 23, 2026
* **Notification of Acceptance:** Before November 7, 2026

**Additional Information to Include**
* Target audience level (master, PhD, postdoc+)
* Data/tools availability for potential hands-on demonstrations
* Preferred presentation format (tutorial, case study, methodology overview)
* Project maturity stage (proof-of-concept, operational, production)
