# 4th Workshop on Quantum Computing @ INFN

We are pleased to announce the 4th Workshop on Quantum Computing @ INFN, which will take place at **DAMA – Tecnopolo Data Manifattura Emilia-Romagna in Bologna**, from **17 to 19 February 2027**.

**Event Link:** https://agenda.infn.it/event/53380/

Quantum computing has the potential to bring about a paradigm shift in research areas of interest to INFN, ranging from quantum machine learning to event reconstruction and simulation in experiments, theoretical physics, and many other fields.

The Quantum Computing @ INFN workshop provides an opportunity for networking and training, with the objectives of presenting ongoing activities, fostering the exchange of knowledge and experience, and attracting researchers and technologists interested in acquiring or further developing their expertise in the field.

The programme, which is currently being defined, will include invited review talks, as well as contributed presentations and posters on ongoing activities. Presentations and posters are reserved for INFN personnel and affiliated members.

To submit a contribution, a short abstract must be submitted via the dedicated link, which will be open from **16 November 2026 to 20 December 2026**.

**Abstract submission:** https://agenda.infn.it/event/53380/abstracts/

The workshop will be held in person, and registration is required to attend.

**Registration:** https://agenda.infn.it/event/53380/registrations/6059/
