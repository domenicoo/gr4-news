From 30 November to 1 December in Bologna, the third edition of the meeting **All around integrability** will take place. This is part of a series of meetings focusing on integrable models and their role in various disciplines, including statistical and condensed matter physics, mathematical physics, pure mathematics, probability, gauge theories, and string theory.

More information can be found at:
[https://rfrassek.github.io/conf/](https://rfrassek.github.io/conf/)

### Scientific Program

*   The meeting will include two minicourses.
*   The remainder of the conference will be devoted to scientific talks and discussions.
*   Applications to give a talk are accepted through the registration form. Due to time constraints, a limited number of contributed talks will be available.
*   Updates on the schedule will appear on the website.
