# International PhD School on: "Theoretical Aspects of Astroparticle Physics, Cosmology and Gravitation"

We gladly announce the 2027 edition of the International PhD School, to be held at the Galileo Galilei Institute of Theoretical Physics (GGI, Florence, Italy) from 1 to 12 March, 2027.

Details can be found at the page: https://agenda.infn.it/event/54324

Applications can be submitted through the GGI web page (deadline: December 20, 2026): https://www.ggi.infn.it/apply-ggi.php?id=569

A limited number of selected PhD students will be admitted.

The School, aimed at first- and second-year PhD students, offers a solid and detailed introduction to the fundamental theoretical concepts and main tools to work in the field of Astroparticle Physics, Cosmology and Gravitation. The courses are organized as lectures at the blackboard in the morning and are integrated with hands-on and discussion sessions with an instructor in the afternoon.

The program of the 2027 edition comprises the following four courses:

* Josef Pradler (Austrian Academy of Sciences and University of Vienna): Dark sectors
* Miguel Escudero (CERN): Cosmological Neutrinos
* Carmelo Evoli (GSSI): Cosmic Rays
* TBD: Theoretical Cosmology and Gravity

The School will profit of the Galileo Galilei Institute facilities. Each student will have office space to study and discuss, with a lot of opportunities to interact with lecturers. Practical information on accommodation, support and meals can be found at the School webpage.
